# The Arcane Spellbook

**English** | [简体中文](README.md)

This catalogue contains the Academy's approved instruction in spell theory, calibration, field computation, and practical examination. It is intended for apprentices, practising mages, and instructors who require more from a spell than a remembered incantation.

A spell is a technical discipline of calculation, movement, and judgement. A qualified student must be able to account for mana expenditure, state why an approximation is valid, recognise the last safe commitment point, and abort cleanly when observation no longer supports a solution.

> Open Academy edition. Mundane visitors may consult the catalogue, but should refrain from independently verifying its conclusions in dormitories, laboratories, or other occupied structures.

## Status of This Edition

The English course is being prepared. The current reviewed spell chapters remain available in the [Simplified Chinese edition](README.md).

English chapters will be catalogued under `spells-en/`. Their authoring standard will be maintained separately under `skills/arcane-spellbook-en/`, so that English instructional prose can be edited as English rather than mechanically transcribed from another language.

Both editions will share the same spell models, notation, numerical examples, validity limits, abort conditions, and examination outcomes. No translation may be admitted to the active syllabus until those elements have passed technical comparison with the source chapter.

## Academy Units

- Mana is the sole energy source of every spell and is measured in the Academy unit $\mathrm{ae}$.
- Ordinary distance, time, temperature, mass, force, and pressure use SI units.
- Mathematical notation uses standard LaTeX.
- Heat, impulse, constraint, motion, and resonance are conversion channels, spell states, or expenditure terms of mana; they are not independent energy sources.
